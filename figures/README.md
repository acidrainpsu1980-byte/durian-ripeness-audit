# figures/

## paper/ — the figures of the manuscript

`Figure1`–`Figure6` (`.pdf` as typeset, `.png` at the resolution used in the Word version) are the
figures exactly as they appear in the manuscript (v2.2.0). The plotted values are rows of the tables
named in `audit/table_map.csv` (Figure 3: `results/fusion_results.csv`; Figure 4:
`results/common_label/leakage_table_v22.csv`; Figure 5: `results/lobo_table.csv`; Figure 6:
`docs/protocol/power_analysis.csv`). Figure 6 is regenerated by `src/protocol/power_analysis.py`
(ANCHOR stage of `run_all.sh`); the composition of Figures 1–5 is not regenerated by the package.

## diagnostic/ — working plots, not the paper figures

`run_all.sh` stage 2 (or `make figures`, i.e. `python src/fusion_figures.py` from the repository root)
writes three diagnostic plots here at 300 dpi:

- `fig_fusion_ablation.png` - session macro-F1 / accuracy per modality subset and fusion rule (5-fold mean, 95 % t-CI); data of Figure 3
- `fig_leakage.png`         - fruit-grouped vs capture-level random split; drawn from the earlier table
  `results/leakage_table.csv`, in which the LightGBM rows were trained on file-name labels. Figure 4 of the
  paper uses `results/common_label/leakage_table_v22.csv` (LightGBM refit on the reconstructed label), so
  several values differ by up to about 2 pp
- `fig_lobo.png`            - leave-one-harvest-batch-out per sensor and fusion vs majority baseline; data of Figure 5

These plots use different model subsets, rounding and titles from the paper figures; quote the paper
figures and the tables, not these plots.
